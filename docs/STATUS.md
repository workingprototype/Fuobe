**Legend**

| | meaning |
|---|---|
| ✅ | **Verified**: run end to end here (unit tests, a live ComfyUI, the browser) |
| 🟢 | **Built**: implemented against verified API schemas or validated workflows, and the UI flow checked in demo mode, but not yet run with real paid keys or real GPU weights |
| 🟡 | **Partial**: works, but approximates Higgsfield or misses part of the feature |
| ⬜ | **Not started** |

**What "verified" can't cover on this machine:** there are no paid API keys and no GPU here. Every cloud model call and every open-source model output is therefore 🟢 at best: request shapes come from the providers' own schemas, and every ComfyUI graph is accepted by a real ComfyUI. The real outputs come from the tools that need no model weights, which ran on a CPU ComfyUI here: joining clips (also the packet-copy join), palette match, trim, cutting a segment, adding a soundtrack (one track, or a voice over a background bed), caption burn-in, the dub voice timeline, joins that keep a clip's sound, extract frame, VHS meta-batch passes, Ken Burns moves, music loops and audio joins with crossfades, VTracer vectorizing, SDR to HDR (HDR10 and EXR frames), pro export (ProRes, H.265 10-bit, FFV1, EXR), the window, sound and work-size parts of the object-removal graphs, Click to Ad's logo end card on the open engine, and the Canvas Video concatenator (each clip cut and framed, then joined). Model steps also ran on CPU: MoGe 2 with its real weights (the 3D scene, and since PR #10 the field-of-view step of the multi-view 3D graph), and FPS Boost (RIFE) and Real-ESRGAN in passes with random-weight stand-ins, so their output quality wasn't judged. App features that need no model (the library, live updates, notifications, sign-in, the Layers editor, browser color grading, the ⌘K palette, Settings → LoRAs, the evaluation harness's report and review pages, the Canvas board's pages, groups, comments and drawings) are verified in the browser, and one LoRA download ran live. Fuobe Soul and Fuobe DoP, the first Fuobe Originals in the app, are 🟢: no look or camera move has been rendered here.

## 3. Known issues and caveats

Most important first.

**Not run for real (no keys, no GPU)**
- **Paid providers are untested with real keys.** That includes round 3's response shapes (Seedream layer boxes, Recraft's returned `style_id`, transcript cues, 3D files, Demucs stems, the fal FFmpeg tools) and round 4, part 1's routes: Topaz Video by size, Starlight, ByteDance and FLUX.3 Upscale, Topaz FPS Boost and SDR to HDR, LTX 2.3 HDR's EXR zip, the Bria erasers, VOID, LTX clean plate, SAM 3.1 masks, hosted VACE, P-Video Replace, Genjutsu and Genjutsu Lite, FLUX 3 Max keyframes, Marigold V2, fal's Ken Burns and merge-audios, and the cloud dub's split, loudnorm and mix. Round 4, part 2 adds: per-frame Mixed Media (how many frames extract-nth-frame returns and in what order, Nano Banana 2 edits with two images, images-to-video at a fractional fps such as 7.5, merge-videos with a set size), the cloud end card (whether merge-videos keeps the ad's voiceover next to the card's silent track, how overlay-image scales a very large logo), Florence-2 OCR (its box units, pixels assumed, and its real price; the $0.001 shown is a placeholder), the Inpaint and Enhancer models, Content Factory's reading (fal frames, Scribe v2, Claude with frames), the AI Influencer sheets, the six hosted LoRA models and every LoRA payload, and civitai downloads and mirrors (mirror lifetimes of 7 days on fal and 1 day on Replicate are guesses). Round 4, part 3 adds: Fuobe Soul on fal and Replicate (fal's rounding to whole megapixels for Qwen-Image 2512 is assumed from its price text, and Replicate is priced at fal's rate), Fuobe DoP on fal's Wan 2.2 A14B and LTX-2.3 and its Replicate fallback, LTX-2.5 Fast's focus shift, the hosted Qwen-Image-Edit 2509 relight, SAM 3D Objects, Meshy multi-view and rigging (and playing a real Meshy animation), Tripo text to 3D, TripoSplat, and the Canvas nodes' ElevenLabs Voice Changer and Dubbing, hosted Chatterbox and the concatenator's fal steps (whether merge-videos keeps sound when one clip has no sound track is unknown). Prices are list prices; ByteDance's 6K and 8K prices are extrapolated from 4K, and Genjutsu's 50% promotion is mentioned, not subtracted. Assumptions from the docs: fal compose cuts an audio keyframe at its `duration` and renders an audio-only timeline over a still; cloud `join-clips` keeps each clip's audio.
- **Open-source quality is unverified.** Round 3 added about 165 GB of model files (plus about 9 GB that nodes download on first use) that have never run here. Round 4, part 1's heavy open workflows were only accepted by ComfyUI, not run: Wan 2.1 VACE (remove, swap, insert), VOID, Wan Animate 2, SeedVR2 in passes, Frames films, the Qwen-Image-Edit 2511 + Multiple-Angles re-render and a full open Faceless render. Round 4, part 3's open routes were only accepted by ComfyUI: every Soul look, Soul Cinema, `z-image-turbo-i2i` and stacks of three LoRAs; Fuobe DoP on Wan 2.2 and the three LTX-2.3 graphs; the relight step; TRELLIS.2 Multi-view, open TripoSplat and the open text-to-3D chain. To check on the first runs: Wan 2.1 LoRAs on Wan 2.2, the LTX-2 19B Earth Zoom Out LoRA on LTX-2.3, the 2509 Relight LoRA on fal's plus-lora endpoint, `camera_lora_scale` on the plain LTX-2.3 endpoint, `focus_shift` on LTX-2.5 Fast, and whether MoGe's estimated field of view helps multi-view models from real phone photos. No LoRA has rendered on a GPU. Whole open chains (a UGC presenter film, a Faceless episode, a dub with lip re-sync, a Shorts restyle, a Content Factory series) ran only on the demo provider.
- **No Fuobe Original has been measured.** Fuobe Soul and Fuobe DoP are in the app, but the evaluation harness ran only on the demo provider and on CPU-only tools; its `soul`, `soul-cinema`, `soul-hex`, `dop` and `dop-mix` suites are ready for GPU and cloud runs. No recipe has been promoted, and the preset covers it can make (`src/lib/preset-previews.generated.ts`) are still empty.

**Gaps that change what a user gets**
- **Home's Start spends money.** Start (or Enter) on Home now runs the job; with a saved key for the default model that is a paid run (for example a Seedance 2.5 clip). The line under the box, which says what will run and what it costs, is the only warning. A link from elsewhere can't start a run.

**Library, data and security**
- "Move to" and "new folder" in the Library's selection bar still act on the whole selection, including results hidden by a search or filter; the count and the other actions follow the shown results.
- Without Web Locks (plain `http://` on a LAN IP) each visible tab reads its own live stream; six or more visible windows on one host could hit the browser's connection limit.
- **Sign-in is off by default.** Without a password Fuobe answers only on local addresses and names in `FUOBE_ALLOWED_ORIGINS`, but anyone who can reach it can use it (and your keys). With a password, a page that rebinds DNS can still post password guesses; the throttle caps that at roughly 15–20 a minute. Without `FUOBE_TRUST_PROXY=1` behind a reverse proxy, all visitors share one throttle (slowed, never locked out). `comfyLorasDir` (now also where LoRAs are downloaded) and `aiToolkitDatasetDir` make the server write files where you point them. Save & test lets a signed-in caller make the server fetch a ComfyUI or Ollama address without saving it (saving it already allowed the same).
- **LoRA downloads through the API:** anyone with a Fuobe API token can name a LoRA link in `POST /api/generations` (at most 8; only links on huggingface.co, hf.co, civitai.com, fal.media and replicate.delivery) and make the server download it into the LoRA folder. Links on hf.co, fal.media and replicate.delivery aren't fingerprint-checked and have no size limit (60-minute timeout).
- **Canvas data format:** a canvas is saved as pages (`{pages:[…]}`) the next time it is saved, and older Fuobe builds can't read it after that; back up the data folder before trying this version if you might go back. Scripts that read `canvas.graph` from `GET /api/canvases/:id` must read `canvas.pages` now. No database migration was needed.
- **Fuobe Soul's finish route** (`POST /api/generations/[id]/finish`) takes the PNG the browser made and puts it in place of the output; the raw picture stays as `meta.original`, the first upload wins, and the route is guarded only by the app's usual sign-in.
- `MAX_INLINE_TEXT` caps a result's inline text at 64,000 characters; `meta.cues` of a very long transcript stays inline.
- The first routing check after the Ollama or ComfyUI URL changes can hold a request for up to 1.5 s when the host doesn't answer.

## 5. How to reproduce the checks

```bash
pnpm install
pnpm typecheck && pnpm lint && pnpm test && pnpm build   # in a checkout without .next, run `pnpm exec next typegen` (or a dev or build run) before typecheck
```

Browser checks need the app running (`pnpm dev`, or `pnpm build && pnpm start`); demo mode with no keys is fine. They use Playwright: the repo's `playwright` / `playwright-core` if it resolves, else a global install, and `$CHROMIUM_PATH` or the newest `/opt/pw-browsers/chromium-*`.

```bash
pnpm test:e2e:mobile http://localhost:3000            # tests/e2e/no-horizontal-scroll.mjs: every route at 390 px, light and dark (--light / --dark, --routes=/a,/b)
pnpm test:e2e:shell http://localhost:3000             # tests/e2e/shell.mjs: skip link, landmarks, ⌘K palette, phone drawer and Search, full-screen studios, settings deep link
pnpm test:e2e:library http://localhost:3000           # tests/e2e/library-flows.mjs: helper steps, pins, review status, nested folders, zips, upload delete, Select all, 390 px (--keep leaves its tagged test data)
node tests/e2e/composer-flows.mjs http://localhost:3000   # first paint, typing before hydration, paste once, upload cancel, phone toolbar and blocker line
node tests/e2e/notifications.mjs http://localhost:3000    # job notifications and the running count in the tab title
node tests/e2e/clipboard.mjs http://localhost:3000        # Copy buttons without the Clipboard API (a plain-http LAN address), read back from the clipboard
node tests/e2e/layers.mjs http://localhost:3000           # Layers regressions (projects page, deep links, edits during a save, open-engine Control)
node tests/e2e/cinema-scene.mjs http://localhost:3000     # 3D scene: ask first, point cloud, fly, Capture → recompose, the MoGe mesh at 390 px (WebGL on SwiftShader)
node tests/e2e/canvas-smoke.mjs http://localhost:3000 --perf=60   # save on leave, rename, trash → re-run, keystroke timing
node tests/e2e/canvas-board.mjs http://localhost:3000     # Canvas tools (draw, shapes, text, comments, eraser), groups and Run group, pages saved apart, Undo per page, page tabs by keyboard, scroll pans
node tests/e2e/canvas-open-dub.mjs http://localhost:3000  # a video Translate node on an open-source-only install; sets Settings → Engine mode for the run and puts it back
node tests/e2e/dop-flows.mjs http://localhost:3000        # Fuobe DoP in the Video studio, the grade hand-off, 390 px light and dark (--shots=dir); start the app with FAL_KEY=anything to add the cloud price checks (nothing is sent)
node tests/e2e/cinema-shortcuts.mjs http://localhost:3000 # Ctrl/Cmd+Enter only from the prompt bar; the new-project name and the phone Director chip
node tests/e2e/assist-flows.mjs http://localhost:3000     # skills menu, remember, projects, skill upload, modes, phone sheet
```

`layers.mjs` and `cinema-scene.mjs` expect their open-engine step to run as a demo: run them while no ComfyUI is reachable. Demo stills are SVG placeholders a real ComfyUI can't read, and the Layers step needs the Qwen-Image-Layered weights.

The evaluation harness ([`scripts/eval/README.md`](../scripts/eval/README.md)):

```bash
pnpm eval:validate                                    # every case file (12 suites, with soul-cinema, soul-hex and dop-mix), and that the generated suites match the research
node tests/e2e/eval-review.mjs                        # the report and blind A/B review pages at 1440 and 390 px, light and dark; builds a small run from fixtures, no app needed (or pass scripts/eval/results/<runId>)
pnpm eval:run --suite selftest --engine demo          # about 30 s, free: against a running Fuobe (http://localhost:3000, or --url / FUOBE_URL) with Settings → Demo mode on Auto or On
pnpm eval:run --suite soul-hex --engine demo          # the same way: Fuobe Soul's palettes, scored by the palette metric (demo pictures, so the scores say nothing about quality)
```

Scoring needs the metrics venv (`pnpm eval:setup`, needs uv) or uv itself; `--no-metrics` skips it. `--engine open` needs a live ComfyUI with the recipe's LoRAs (a GPU for anything but the self-test), and `--engine cloud` a fal or Replicate key, under a spend cap.

The open-source engine against a live ComfyUI (no model weights needed):

```bash
bash scripts/comfy/install-comfyui.sh           # ComfyUI v0.41 + 15 custom node packs at pinned commits (CPU; NC_PACKS=1 adds ComfyUI-Whisper)
bash scripts/comfy/start-comfyui.sh             # 127.0.0.1:8188, or point COMFYUI_URL at your own server
pnpm build && pnpm start &
pnpm test:e2e:comfyui http://localhost:3000     # tests/e2e/comfyui-selftest.mjs: self-tests, mux (one track and voice + bed), meta-batch, trim, segment, captions; a 600-frame clip with RSS sampling; ffprobe/ffmpeg checks when installed
node tests/e2e/comfyui-faceless-plus.mjs http://localhost:3000   # Ken Burns, the packet-copy join, a crossfaded loop and join; needs ffmpeg / ffprobe on PATH, and demo mode off (or nothing else configured); on Linux it also samples ComfyUI's memory (found with pgrep, or set COMFYUI_PID)
node tests/e2e/comfyui-canvas-concat.mjs http://localhost:3000   # the Canvas Video concatenator through the canvas API: three clips made with ffmpeg, cut and framed to 9:16, joined, then probed; model-free, no cloud keys needed
```

Re-export and re-validate the workflow library (needs ComfyUI running; never runs a model):

```bash
npm run build -- <ids> && node validate-workflows.mjs <ids>   # single workflows; always pass ids in a fresh checkout without build/raw/

```

The `fuobe` CLI is covered by `pnpm test`; to use it against a running server, see [`docs/cli.md`](cli.md) (`npm link`, or `node bin/fuobe.mjs …`).
