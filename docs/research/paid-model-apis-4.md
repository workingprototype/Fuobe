# Paid model APIs, part 4: video erase and swap, FPS / HDR / codecs, FFmpeg compose, OCR, 3D scenes and new models

As of **2026-10-22**. This extends [`paid-model-apis.md`](./paid-model-apis.md), [`-2.md`](./paid-model-apis-2.md) and [`-3.md`](./paid-model-apis-3.md). It covers the paid endpoints behind the round-4 features: removing an object from a video, swapping or inserting one from a reference image, FPS boost and slow motion, SDR to HDR, ProRes export, FFmpeg-style composition (Ken Burns, slideshows, end cards, audio loops), OCR with boxes for Layers, Cinema Studio's 3D scene and recomposition, and the models released between 2026-09-15 and 2026-10-22.

The machine-readable companion is [`paid-model-apis-4.json`](./paid-model-apis-4.json). It holds **114 endpoint records**: 89 fal, 22 Replicate, 2 Higgsfield and 1 World Labs. 14 of them re-verify endpoints that parts 1-3 already had (current schema, `outputFields`, new key-field slots); the other 100 are new (three of them added by the catalog package, §14). It has the same top-level keys as part 3 (`asOf`, `method`, `conventions`, `recommendations`, `gaps`, `counts`, `endpoints`) and the same record fields.

## How this was verified

- **fal catalog.** The full catalog at `https://api.fal.ai/v1/models` (paged) had **1,527 active endpoints** on 2026-10-22 (1,516 on 2026-10-17). 105 carry a catalog date of 2026-09-15 or later. Some of those are re-dated older apps, so every candidate was checked against parts 1-3 before it counted as new.
- **fal schemas.** 225 endpoint ids (catalog hits plus guessed ids) were probed at `https://fal.ai/api/openapi/queue/openapi.json?endpoint_id=<id>`. 153 served a schema and 72 did not (the misses are listed in section 11). Schemas were parsed by script with part 3's rules: refs resolved, `anyOf` with null turned into `nullable`, nested item and field schemas kept, and the output schema kept as `outputFields`. A JSON-schema `const` is recorded as a one-value enum.
- **Parser check.** The parser re-read 58 endpoints that parts 1-3 already recorded and reproduced their inputs. The only real drift is two added fields (`enable_safety_checker` on the Topaz video upscalers, `sync_mode` on FLUX.3 klein). Kling O3's `shot_type` is a `const` that part 1 wrote as a plain string; it did not change.
- **fal prices** come from the `## Pricing` section of `https://fal.ai/models/<id>/llms.txt`. Titles, licences and status come from `?endpoint_id=` catalog lookups. *Unlisted* means the schema is served but the id is not in the catalog.
- **Replicate.** Candidates came from the search API (`https://replicate.com/api/search`) and the newest-models feed (`https://replicate.com/api/latest-models?page=1..12`). Schemas, billing and the latest version come from the `dereferenced_openapi_schema`, `billingConfig` and `version` embedded in each model page (38 pages fetched).
- **Higgsfield.** The pricing API (`https://dash.higgsfield.ai/api/v2/pricing/models/?page=1..4`, 32 families) was compared with [`higgsfield-api.json`](./higgsfield-api.json). The one new family's schema comes from `https://dash.higgsfield.ai/models/<id>/llms.txt`.
- **World Labs.** `https://docs.worldlabs.ai/api/reference/openapi.yaml` and `https://docs.worldlabs.ai/api/pricing.md`.
- **Licences** of community models come from the upstream `LICENSE` files or Hugging Face model cards.
- **Bria's mask polarity** is not in its schema. It was inferred by measuring Bria's own example mask video with ffmpeg: 1280x704, 24 fps, 121 frames; frame 10 is 96.0% black and 1.7% white, so white marks the object.
- **No paid inference calls were made.** Anything the schemas do not state is marked *unverified*.

### Loading it into the catalog

`scripts/gen-cloud-models.py` merges part 4 the same way as part 3, with four lines after the part 3 loop:

```python
RESEARCH_4 = json.loads((ROOT / "docs/research/paid-model-apis-4.json").read_text())
for e in RESEARCH_4["endpoints"]:
    prev = EPS.get((e["provider"], e["endpoint"]))
    EPS[(e["provider"], e["endpoint"])] = {**e, "keyFields": {**((prev or {}).get("keyFields") or {}), **(e.get("keyFields") or {})}}
```

This was checked on a scratch copy of the script:

- With part 4 merged, `cloud-models.generated.ts` comes out **byte-identical** (147 models). The 14 re-verified records only add key-field slots.
- 15 probe models on new endpoints built routes with no warnings: FLUX 3 Max (t2v, i2v and an automatic start/end-frame route), HeyGen Video (t2v, i2v, ref2v with 9 images / 3 videos / 3 audios), Grok Imagine Video 1.5 and Image 2.0, Krea 3 (fal and Replicate), HeyGen Voice Turbo (voice list and cloning picked up), Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS (30 voices), PixVerse V6 Max (Replicate), the Bria eraser (fal and Replicate), Topaz interpolate and SDR to HDR, Genjutsu Light (provider `higgsfield`), Kling 4.0 edit, TripoSplat, Florence-2 OCR and the Ken Burns utility.
- Two price traps: `parse_price` reads FLUX 3 Max's promotional $0.0425/s (it rises to $0.17/s after 2026-11-07), and finds nothing in Grok's "480p at $0.08/sec" wording. Give those models an explicit `pricing` tuple.
- The new key-field slots (listed in `conventions.newKeyFieldSlots`, e.g. `codec`, `maskPrompt`, `slowdown`, `zoom`, `overlayVideo`) are not read by `build_route`. Studio code should read them from the route's research record, as with part 3.
- The two Higgsfield records follow `higgsfield-api.json`'s request lifecycle, but `gen-higgsfield-models.py` reads only that file, so copy them there to use them. Fuobe has no `worldlabs` provider yet.

## 2. What changed upstream since part 3 (2026-10-17)

- **Higgsfield** added one family to its public API: **Genjutsu Light** (released 2026-10-20), with Motion Transfer and Object Swap modes. It has the same inputs as Genjutsu but uses only the first 15 s of the source and costs $0.05 / $0.10 / $0.20 per second at 480p / 720p / 1080p, against Genjutsu's $0.318 / $0.681 / $1.632. Both show 50% off on 2026-10-22. Nothing else changed: there is still no public endpoint for Cinema Studio's 3D scene, remove object, swap object, FPS Boost or the Hyperion upscale.
- **Higgsfield's own pricing for these features** (Cinema Studio, from [`higgsfield-studios.json`](./higgsfield-studios.json)): `remove_object`, `swap_object` and `draw_to_video` all cost 3 / 4.5 / 12 credits per second at 480p / 720p / 1080p plus 3.5 "frame credits". Pricing them like Draw to Video suggests the same pipeline: edit the first frame, then let a video model carry the edit through the clip *(inferred)*.
- **Higgsfield's Upscale** lists ByteDance (up to 60 fps), Topaz Video with Hyperion 2.5, FLUX 3, FPS Boost to 24/30/60/90/120 and exports to H.264, H.265 10-bit, ProRes 10-bit and EXR 16-bit ([`higgsfield-inventory.json`](./higgsfield-inventory.json)). Topaz's interpolation and Hyperion SDR-to-HDR are now on fal (section 5).
- **Kling 4.0** appeared in fal's catalog on 2026-10-16 (text, image, reference and edit). Every page says *"Kling 4.0 is available in the playground and fal Agent. API access is coming soon."* The records carry status `playground-only`. Higgsfield's web app has offered Kling 4.0 since 2026-10-21 (per [`higgsfield-delta-4.md`](./higgsfield-delta-4.md)), but Higgsfield's public pricing API does not list it, so no API reaches it yet.
- **New unlisted fal utilities**: `fal-ai/workflow-utilities/image-to-video` (Ken Burns zoom), `overlay-image`, `overlay-video` and `video-to-gif`. They are served but not in the catalog, so they could change without notice.
- **Pre-release ids**: fal serves OpenAPI documents for `pixverse/v6-max/{text,image}-to-video` (and the same under `fal-ai/`) with no catalog entry and a $0 placeholder price. Replicate already sells PixVerse V6 Max (`pixverse/pixverse-v6-max`, version 2026-10-21).

**What Higgsfield does.** You click "Create 3D scene" (*"This will render a 3D scene from your current image."*), move the image across three axes, change the perspective, pick a keyframe, and "recomposition" re-renders it (`source_job_id`, the image and an aspect ratio; 2 credits). **Cloud recipe (default):**

1. `fal-ai/marigold-v2` with `prediction_type: depth` ($0.03) gives a depth map of the whole frame.
2. The browser builds a point cloud from the image and the depth. The user moves the camera, as in Higgsfield.
3. Fuobe turns the chosen camera into `horizontal_angle`, `vertical_angle` and `zoom`. `fal-ai/qwen-image-edit-2511-multiple-angles` (already in the catalog) re-renders the still from there. For a freer re-render, send the original plus the rough point-cloud view to Nano Banana Pro edit.

This is the cloud twin of the open plan (Depth Anything 3 point cloud, then Qwen-Image-Edit-2511 with the Multiple-Angles LoRA). It costs about $0.07 per recomposition. *Critic note: the open side now has a better first step. ComfyUI core's MoGe nodes turn the photo into a textured GLB mesh of the whole scene (`scene-mesh-moge`, ran on CPU in 42 s for 768×768; [`comfyui-oss-4.md`](./comfyui-oss-4.md) §i), which the browser can orbit like Higgsfield's splat. Core ComfyUI also has native TripoSplat nodes, but, as stated below, TripoSplat is subject-only.*

**A real 3D world.** World Labs Marble (`POST https://api.worldlabs.ai/marble/v1/worlds:generate`) builds an explorable world as SPZ Gaussian splats from one image: $1.264 with `marble-1.1`, $0.184 as a draft. It needs a separate World Labs key and credits, and Fuobe needs a small provider for it (async operation polling).

**What does not fit.** TripoSplat removes the background first (its output schema calls `preprocessed_image` "the background-removed RGB composite the model actually saw"), so it rebuilds the subject, not the scene. `higgsfield-delta-4.md` suggests it for this feature; on this evidence it suits product spins and subject placement instead. A whole-scene splat needs Marble, or splats built in the browser from the depth map. SAM 3D Objects rebuilds objects with their placement but no background. The fal "worlds" models are realtime WebRTC sessions, not queue jobs. Apple SHARP is research-only.

## 10. Quirks to encode in the adapter

- **Bria video endpoints** reject or trim clips of 5 s or more. On fal, `auto_trim` defaults to **true** (silently cut to 5 s); on Replicate it defaults to **false** (rejected). The output `video` is a Video|File union: read `.url`. `keypoints` are **strings** such as `"{'x':100,'y':100,'type':'positive'}"`.
- **Masks:** Bria uses white = erase *(inferred)*; LTX inpaint uses white = regenerate, black = keep (stated); MiniMax-Remover uses white = remove (stated); VOID's `quad_mask_video_url` is its own 4-value format, so send `mask_prompt` instead unless Fuobe builds quad masks.
- **VOID** needs `prompt` (the scene after removal) and caps `num_frames` at 197.
- **Kling 4.0** has status `playground-only`: do not route to it until the page drops "API access is coming soon". Its edit and video-reference prices bill input + output seconds (at least 2x the output).
- **Wan Animate Replace (fal)** bills "video seconds" as frames ÷ 16, so a 5 s clip at 60 fps bills 18.75 s.
- **Genjutsu Light** `duration` is read-only (taken from the source, 4-15 s); billable seconds include reference video length.
- **Topaz interpolate** outputs H.265 unless `H264_output: true`. **Topaz SDR to HDR** `prores` files are much larger than `mp4`.
- **`bria/video/increase-resolution`** `desired_increase` is a **string** enum `"2"|"4"`; its default codec is `webm_vp9`, so send `mp4_h264` for playback. **Kandinsky VSR** `upscale_factor` is a **number** enum 2 | 2.25 | 4.
- **`workflow-utilities/image-to-video`**: `zoom_factor` minimum is 1 (no zoom). **`video-to-gif`**: at most 15 s.
- **Moondream 3 detect** returns boxes normalised to 0-1 and no text. **Florence-2** `label` is the line text.
- **SAM 3D Objects** `prompt` defaults to `'car'`: always send it.
- **HunyuanWorld image-to-world** requires `labels_fg1`, `labels_fg2` and `classes` strings whose format is undocumented.
- **FLUX 3 Max and H3 Max camera controls** are at promotional prices that end on 2026-11-07 and 2026-10-31.
- **Gemini 3.8 TTS dialogue:** omit `prompt` and send exactly two `speakers` plus `turns`.
- **World Labs:** auth header `WLT-Api-Key`; generation is an operation to poll; image URLs on hotlink-protected hosts fail with 400 (upload through `media-assets:prepare_upload`).
