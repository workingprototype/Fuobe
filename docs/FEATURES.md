# Fuobe features

[← README](../README.md) · [Setup and self-hosting](SELF-HOSTING.md) · [Development](DEVELOPMENT.md) · [Status](STATUS.md)

Fuobe is a complete creative studio you can run yourself: 108 camera motions, 255 effects and 132 apps, Cinema, Shorts, Marketing and Ads studios, Layers, Canvas, Storyboard, Image to 3D, voice and music tools, and an Assist agent that plans and runs whole jobs. Every tool runs either on open-weight models on your own GPU through **ComfyUI**, or on frontier models with your own fal.ai / Replicate key. Flip one switch. Licences are information, not gates: **Settings → Models & licences** shows the licence of every model, LoRA and node pack.

**Every preview on the site was rendered locally on ComfyUI** with open weights: stills with Ideogram 4, before/after end frames with Qwen Image 2.1 edits, and the motion clips with MiniMax H3 (including its Orbit LoRA for bullet time), all on one RTX 5090.

## Assist, skills, MCP and the command line

- **Assist:** describe the whole job in a chat; the agent picks models, shows the plan and its cost, runs it when you approve, checks the results and chains the next step. Free / Smart / Manual modes, 21 built-in **skills** (SKILL.md playbooks, or upload your own), **projects** with instructions and up to 20 sources (links, files and PDFs, elements, notes), and **memory**. **Scheduled tasks** run a request daily, weekly, every 1–168 hours or once, in your time zone; each run is a new chat that asks before it generates, or runs on its own within a cost cap ($2 a run by default). Your own **MCP connectors** (remote MCP servers, added in Settings → Assist · connectors) give Claude chats another app's tools: Anthropic's servers call them, so a local one needs a public tunnel, and their tools run without asking Fuobe, so each connector's Allowed tools list is the safeguard. Runs on Claude, a local model (Ollama, LM Studio, Unsloth Studio or another OpenAI-compatible server), or an offline planner (connectors need Claude)
- **Skills that run by themselves:** **Make a skill from this…** (the ⋯ menu on a result) or **Make a skill** (the Library's selection bar) turns the steps behind your results, Edit and Animate chains made by hand included, into a skill of your own: pick the inputs that change next time, give it a free **Test run**, save it. In **Assist → Skills**, a skill with a recipe (`recipe.json`: its steps and inputs) says "Runs by itself" and gets a **Run…** form: typed inputs, the price before it starts, the steps, and a Many tab for up to 50 CSV rows. No chat or AI is involved. A skill must be priced on an engine (part of the free **Test run**) before it runs there; the test run then runs every step once with placeholder results (a studio tool step on its own is priced only; beside other steps it runs as a demo too), so nothing runs for real and nothing is spent. It asks for a new test run (**Test again**) when a model it uses is retired, switched off or repriced. ⌘K has "Run a skill", and a skill downloads as a .zip with its files. Translate & dub, Katana and Captions results can become skills; results from other studios are refused for now, naming the studio
- **MCP:** other agents (Claude Code, Claude Desktop, any MCP client) can drive the studio through `/api/mcp`, including whole studio jobs that open in their studio (a Layers project, a Shorts session, a dub to review, a Faceless episode, an Ads Studio batch, the Captions editor, a Katana edit; Genjutsu, the Character Swap Studio, Remove object, Content Factory, the AI Influencer builder, Evolution and Layers' Inpaint, Enhancer and Save as element aren't tools yet) and the skills as prompts. `npx skills add workingprototype/Fuobe` installs the skills in other agents
- **Command line:** `fuobe` lists models, prices a run, generates, waits and downloads from a terminal, a script or a coding agent; see [`docs/cli.md`](cli.md). `--engine` also picks an Original's engine, and `--look` a Fuobe Soul look (`fuobe generate --help` lists it; cli.md doesn't yet)

## Fuobe Originals

**Fuobe Originals:** each is one model that runs on both engines, built on open-weight models and public LoRAs: free on your GPU through ComfyUI, or paid on fal or Replicate

- **Fuobe Soul**, **Fuobe Soul HD** and **Fuobe Soul Cinema** (Image studio) — a Look picker with 139 looks in two sets named after the parity studio's versions (Soul 2.0: 33, Soul: 106), with categories, search, drawn covers and a Strength slider. Attach a picture and Fuobe Soul restyles it with the look. Soul HD (Qwen-Image 2512) is for lettering; Soul Cinema has one film look and 21:9. Your browser finishes each result (crop, grade, overlays such as DigitalCam's date stamp) and keeps the raw picture. 87 looks use civitai LoRAs: without a civitai key, a run leaves those out and says so. The PixeletedFace look pixelates faces in a second "Face mosaic" step (SAM 3.1; free on your GPU, $0.01 a picture in the cloud), and the picture before it stays in the Library as a step. The Storyboard's Look chip puts any of the 139 looks on a board
- **Fuobe DoP** (Wan 2.2) and **Fuobe DoP · LTX** (LTX-2.3) — the camera motions and effects as recipes: a move's LoRAs, a camera LoRA or (on your GPU) a Fun Camera pose, a colour grade or a relight where it has one, else its words. On the open engine, picking a move in the Video studio switches to the model it runs on (a few run on LTX-2.5). In the cloud, 184 of the 363 motions and effects use DoP's routes and switch the same way; the other 179 only add their words to the model you picked (their own route is Kling 2.5 Turbo). 15 colour-grade effects hand the clip to Color grading with the grade picked, 6 relight effects relight the start frame first, the 32 Mixed Media effects first draw the start frame in their look on Fuobe Mixed Media (on both engines; in the cloud they ran on Kling 2.5 Turbo before PR #14), and Mix stacks both presets' LoRAs on either engine
- **Fuobe Cinema** (Cinema Studio's video default) — Wan 2.2 14B Lightning free on your GPU (3 or 5 s, no sound), or LTX-2.3 on fal with sound and its built-in camera LoRAs (4–30 s: one call makes up to 20 s, and a longer take gets an automatic extend). In the cloud, 14 of Cinema's 33 camera moves use LTX-2.3's camera LoRA, start frame or not; with a start frame, a move with a Wan 2.2 LoRA switches the take to Fuobe DoP, and Rack Focus uses LTX-2.5 Fast's focus move. On your GPU, with a start frame, a move takes its Wan 2.2 LoRA or a Fun Camera pose. Otherwise the move goes into the prompt
- **Fuobe Genjutsu** (Motion, Swap and Restyle; Motion and Swap also come in a cheaper, faster Standard quality) and **Fuobe Character swap** — Genjutsu's defaults: Wan Animate for motion transfer, SAM 3.1 and Wan VACE for object swap, Wan 2.2 Fun VACE for restyle, and MiniMax H3 with Akatz Labs' Character Swap LoRA for Character swap
- **Fuobe Soul ID** — train a character or a look as a LoRA, free on your GPU through AI-Toolkit or on fal's trainers (see Elements below)
- **Fuobe Marketing Image** (Marketing Studio and Ads Studio) and **Fuobe Try-On** (Fashion Factory) — Marketing Image is Qwen-Image-Edit 2511 with your product photo (Qwen-Image 2512 without one), and your browser sets the headline, CTA and logo over the picture in the brand fonts. Try-On is FLUX.2 [klein] 4B with a try-on LoRA ($0.03 a picture in the cloud)
- **Fuobe Mixed Media** and **Fuobe Shorts** — Mixed Media redraws every frame with Z-Image Turbo along its outlines, with the look's LoRA and one seed (up to 240 frames; about $0.01 a frame at 1K in the cloud, 16 GB on your GPU). Its Smooth mode draws the first frame in the look and lets Wan 2.2 Fun VACE carry it through by depth. Shorts restyles each segment's first frame with Qwen-Image-Edit 2511, then Wan 2.2 Fun VACE carries it through and the segments are joined under the clip's sound (about $0.19 a second for a 30 fps clip at 720p in the cloud, plus the first frames; 24 GB on your GPU)
- **Fuobe Evolution** (Video tools → Evolution) — changes a 4–30 s clip by Low, Medium or High toward a creative direction and up to 40 reference images, priced first, with a before/after wipe and the clip's own sound. On your GPU, Wan 2.2 Fun VACE starts from the clip (24 GB); in the cloud it runs fal's LTX-2.3 video-to-video, a different model that doesn't see the references. "Write it for me" writes the direction with Claude, an Ollama vision model or an offline writer
- **Fuobe Angles** (a beta app beside Angles) — the same shot from another camera (rotate, height, distance, lens) on Qwen-Image-Edit 2509 with a Multiple-angles LoRA: $0.035 in the cloud, free on your GPU (24 GB). The Angles app keeps its recipe. **Fuobe Speak** (InfiniteTalk) and **Fuobe Speak · Motion** (Wan 2.2 speech to video) are in Lipsync's model menu on both engines; Lipsync's defaults don't change
- Built and checked in demo mode, by unit tests and, for their GPU graphs, by a CPU ComfyUI that accepts them without running weights; no look, move, take, swap, frame, clip or training has run on a GPU or with a paid key yet, so their quality and real bills are unmeasured, and the three MiniMax H3 graphs have never loaded in a ComfyUI

## Create

- **Create:** Image · Video (text, image, start/end frame, **Frames**: up to 10 stills on a timeline, reference images and clips, multi-shot, draft mode) · Edit · **Layers** (split a flat image into editable layers, edit text with the lines found for you on either engine, Inpaint a painted area, an Enhancer (skin, soft glow, sharpen, detail), regional edits, save a layer as an element, cropped or positioned zips) · Inpaint & Draw to Edit · Color grading · Camera motions and Effects (on Fuobe DoP) · Storyboard (Popcorn: a board in one of Fuobe Soul's looks if you like, priced next to Generate, and turned into a film; cloud boards start on hosted open models, Z-Image Turbo or Qwen-Image-Edit, then Qwen-Image-Edit 2509 with a next-scene LoRA, and "Frames made on" switches them to Nano Banana Pro) · **Canvas** (wire prompts, files and models into node workflows; every template in a cloud and an open-source version, 10 Effects 2.0 chains among them (your photo, a "Set the scene" edit, then a 5 s Fuobe DoP clip); up to 20 pages per board, groups you can run as one, comment pins with replies, and drawing, shapes and text from a toolbar with keys; a Table node runs the node it feeds once per row; Change voice, Clone voice, Translate (text, or a dub of a video) and Video concatenator (cut, mute and join up to 50 clips) nodes) · Apps
- **Live edit** (Create → Live edit, the Edit studio's Live switch, a Library picture's "Change it as you type") — the picture changes as you type, on your own GPU: Qwen-Image 2.1 with linoyts' 2-step DMD2 LoRA on Fuobe's live nodes (fuobe_live.py, which the Windows start file and the setup card add to ComfyUI). Live frames come at 384 to 768 px (Auto steps down on a slower GPU); a pause renders the text on screen at full quality. One prompt keeps growing: Enter ends a change and starts the next, and every frame renders all the words on the original with one seed for the session, so the picture stays steady while you type and nothing wears down, however many changes you make. Undo drops the newest change; Start fresh makes the picture on screen the new original (a lossless full-size master) and clears the words. Hold C to compare (Shift+C with the original), paint a region to change only that. On "Use the ComfyUI on this computer" the tab talks to its ComfyUI directly. Guests see a recorded session. Non-commercial weights; none of it has rendered on a real GPU yet
- **MOSS-Image** (Image studio → model menu → MOSS-Image) — OpenMOSS's picture model, made for Chinese and English text in pictures. In the cloud it runs on fal at $0.05 per megapixel, up to 4 images (fal's safety checker returns a black image when it flags an output). On your GPU it is free, on ComfyUI's built-in nodes, with about 15 GB of files and 16 GB of VRAM (a guess); **Options → Quality** picks Standard (25 steps), Best (50), Fast (8 steps, an extra 5.4 GB file) or Base model (MOSS-Image Base, 50 steps, an extra 5.4 GB file). Its text encoder is the int8 Qwen3.5-9B: the w4a8 build in ComfyUI's template crashed the graphics driver on a test PC. The Image studio's controls chip now reads **Options** (it read "Director"). Text placed in boxes: the Image studio's Layout mode draws the boxes on the picture, and `src/lib/layout-prompt.ts` turns them into MOSS's prompt
- **Film look** (Image studio and Canvas, MOSS-Image → Film look) — film and camera looks for MOSS-Image on both engines, in eight groups (colour film, slide, black and white, instant, motion picture, toy and odd cameras, grades, broken film), each with a thumbnail from its own test render. The server adds the picked look when the picture is made: the look's words before and after the prompt, and the look's opposite in the negative prompt beside MOSS-Image's own. A black-and-white look first takes the colour words out of the prompt (an LLM rewrite, then a word list; the word list alone when no LLM answers). The prompt box keeps the person's words, and Reuse applies the look again. Enhance, Assist (`film_look`) and the `moss-film-looks` skill write MOSS prompts by the same rules. Looks live in `src/lib/film-looks.ts`; a layout runs without a look
- **The newest open image models, first on your GPU** — Ideogram 4, Qwen-Image 2.1 and its Turbo, Krea 2 (Realism, Turbo, Edit, Identity Edit, Image to image, Inpaint) and FIBO 2 (Dev, Edit Turbo), from the owner's ComfyUI workflows. On the open engine the Image studio opens on Auto (next item) and Edit and Inpaint on Qwen-Image 2.1; every model menu's "Best" list puts the new models first (text to image: Ideogram 4, Qwen-Image 2.1, Krea 2 Realism, MOSS-Image, then FIBO 2 Dev; edits: Qwen-Image 2.1, its Turbo, FIBO 2 Dev and Edit Turbo, then Krea 2 Edit), and so do apps, Layers, Canvas, Cinema stills, Photodump, Fashion Factory and Character swap's anchor. Apps and tools that run a LoRA trained on an older model keep it (Angles, Relight, Product shot, Try-On, Cinema's recompose). Ideogram 4 reads a JSON caption, which Fuobe writes from your words (with your local LLM, never a paid API; a plain caption without one) and keeps as the run's prompt. When Ideogram's safety filter sends its grey card for every picture of a run, the run is made once more with a new seed, then with Qwen-Image 2.1, before it fails and says what to try; the card is kept for the owner to look at. Soul Location doesn't offer Ideogram 4 (pictures of empty places hit that filter most), and a new Layers layer starts on Qwen-Image 2.1. Qwen-Image 2.1, FIBO 2 and Krea 2 Edit take up to 4 references (Krea 2 Edit 2) and leave out the ones you don't give; an edit keeps image 1's shape and size, so Edit and Inpaint show no aspect or size choice for them, and Qwen-Image 2.1's inpaint and expand take the picture alone. An app run made before an app moved to Qwen-Image 2.1 (a retry, Run again, Reuse) runs on Qwen-Image 2.1 and says so. About 92 GB of new files, 20–24 GB of VRAM (estimates), ComfyUI 0.42.0; Krea 2 runs Comfy-Org's fp8 Krea 2 Turbo, the file the owner's edit workflow loads. Krea 2 Inpaint also needs the ComfyUI-Krea2-Ostris-Edit node pack, so it isn't in Inpaint's "Best" list. Qwen-Image 2.1, Ideogram 4 and FIBO 2 weights are non-commercial. None of the 16 graphs has rendered through Fuobe yet
- **Auto, the Image studio's first pick on your GPU** — Auto picks the open model for each run, for everyone (the owner, accounts, and a guest's draft): a run with a picture to work from goes to the edit model for its operation (Qwen-Image 2.1), every prompt from words to MiniMax H3 Image first, where the GPU the run goes to has the VRAM its lane needs (about 32 GB) and its files; then lettering, posters, logos and signs (real words asked for, such as a poster that says "OPEN") to MOSS-Image, which follows the words and draws them in place, then Krea 2 Realism; and every other prompt to Krea 2 Realism, then MOSS-Image. On a smaller GPU, or one without MiniMax H3 Image's files, the run starts on the next model. Auto's 1K and 1.5K run at MiniMax H3 Image's 2K. Auto never picks Ideogram 4 or FIBO 2 Dev for a picture from words; both stay in the model menus to pick by hand. A list the owner edits in Settings → Auto image model (one word, phrase or pattern per line; a built-in list to start with) starts on Krea 2 Realism instead (then MiniMax H3 Image, then MOSS-Image), and the run details name the line it matched. When Auto's first model is switched off or can't run there, the run is planned on the next; when it fails, the next one makes the same run, each model at most once; it is still one run for limits and billing, with nothing charged again. The tile, the run details and the Library say what made it ("Auto · made with Krea 2 Realism", or "Auto · made with MOSS-Image · Auto's next model" when the other one made it), and the price before Run is the picked model's. Picking a model by name always runs that model; when Ideogram 4 picked by name declines a run, Krea 2 Realism, FIBO 2 Dev, then MOSS-Image make it, and the owner sees the words it declined and "Add a pattern", which puts a line on that list
- **Kandinsky 6.0** (Video studio → model menu, both engines) — Kandinsky Lab's model makes 5 s clips at 480p with sound and lip-synced dialogue, from words or a first frame. Words in quotes are what someone says, and a closing `Sound:` line sets the sound. On your GPU it is free, on Kandinsky Lab's own node packs: **Options → Quality** picks Lite (10 steps, the default; about 24 GB of VRAM, an estimate) or Pro (about 80 GB, with an extra 60 GB file), and the Prompt helper (an extra 19 GB file) writes a fuller caption first. In the cloud it runs on fal: Pro or Lite at Fast, Standard or Best steps (Pro · Standard, the default, $0.56 a clip; Lite from $0.08; Pro · Best $1.35). Lipsync & avatars links a talking character from words to the Video studio on Kandinsky. **Kandinsky VSR** (Upscale → Video, both engines) upscales clips up to 5 s by 2x, 2.25x (the default, 480p to 1080p) or 4x, about $0.09 for 5 s of 480p to 1080p on fal
- **Pika H3 Flex** (your GPU only) — Pika's add-on that makes MiniMax H3 clips in 6 steps. In the Video studio, MiniMax H3's Quality offers **Fast · Pika Flex (6 steps)** for words and first and last frames, and a **References** chip takes up to 9 images, 3 clips and 3 voice or music clips (Image N, Clip N and Voice N in the prompt). Cinema's reference takes and "Talk from a voice clip" in Lipsync & avatars use it too. 32 GB of VRAM recorded, not measured. No hosted service offers the add-on, so the cloud keeps hosted MiniMax H3 under its own name. MiniMax H3's other choices now read "Standard · 8 steps" and "Sharper · 20 steps"
- **One sheet** (Storyboard) and **Re-render from layers** (Layers) — One sheet draws up to 8 panels on one picture with MOSS-Image (your GPU or fal), then cuts it into frames, each with Animate and Edit; it stays the owner's until a GPU test is recorded. Re-render from layers (desktop) repaints the layers as one picture, which lands as a new top layer: MOSS-Image on both engines, Ideogram 4.5 and FLUX.3 in the cloud

## Studios

- **Cinema Studio** — projects and scenes, Director's Panel (camera, lens, 50 palettes, lighting rig, Emotion Wheel), 33 camera moves (# in the prompt bar), AI Director, image grids, Soul Cast and Soul Location, and **3D scenes**: turn a still into a scene you fly through, set the lens and frame, and Capture a re-rendered take from that view. Takes run on Fuobe Cinema by default on both engines (Seedance 2.5, Kling 3.0 and Veo 3.1 stay in the cloud menu, LTX-2.5 and Wan 2.2 14B on your GPU), stills on Fuobe Soul Cinema, Soul Cast on Fuobe Soul in the cloud (the Soul Cast sheet on your GPU) and Soul Location on Fuobe Soul. A project can be shared as a read-only link
- **Fly path** (Cinema → a project → a 3D scene take) — film the still along a camera path. The still's own view is keyframe 1: fly the camera and press **+ Keyframe** (or K), up to 24 keyframes in all (in the cloud H3 Max's camera controls take 12, so a longer path is simplified to the 12 that matter most there, and the run says so), or pick one of ten moves (orbits and arcs, a tilted ring, a figure-8, dollies and trucks). **Preview** the flight, then **Film this path**: it lands as a new "Fly path" take. In the cloud, MiniMax H3 Max camera controls film 3–15 s at 480P to 1080P (5 s at 768P costs $0.40, or $0.26 at fal's launch price through 2026-10-31; 15 s at 1080P costs $2.40). On your GPU, Wan 2.1 + Uni3C films about 5 s (81 frames at 16 fps) at 480p for free, guided by your browser's render of the scene along the path (about 48 GB of VRAM), or WorldCast films it while a WorldCast worker answers (below)
- **Props in the 3D scene** (Cinema → a 3D scene → **Add a prop**) — place a splat from the Library (Idol 3D or TripoSplat, from either engine), or make one from a picture with Idol 3D on your GPU. Move, turn and size it with its handles; it shows in captures, blockouts and the Fly path filmed on your GPU. The cloud Fly path and WorldCast film the still, so props don't show there, and the panel says so. Props are kept for the scene in that browser only
- **World mode** (Cinema → the **World** chip, or `/cinema?world=1`; your GPU only) — describe a world, then steer it turn by turn with words and camera moves: 3–12 s a turn, up to 10 turns and 2 minutes in all, with a preview of the camera path, Run turn and Redo from here. **Export** joins the turns into one silent film, with links to Sound for video and Music. It runs Hy-Nomas (1280 × 704 at 24 fps) through the ComfyUI-HyNomas pack; its decoder is non-commercial, and its 24 GB of VRAM is a guess. Nobody hosts it, so the cloud engine says it runs on your GPU, and Paid APIs only hides the chip. Assist and MCP get `world_film`
- **MiniMax H3 at 2K** (Cinema) — a MiniMax H3 take on fal can be 2K, and a finished fal take offers **Finish in 2K** ($0.13 a second), once per take. On your GPU the same 2K tier (a 768p draft finished by Open-H3 Regenerate) is built but held with Regenerate until Regenerate has run on a card that fits it
- **Shorts Studio** — restyle up to 2 minutes of your own footage with 44 looks (or your own references), in segments you can regenerate, stitched back under your sound, on Fuobe Shorts by default on both engines (Gemini Omni Flash and the other partner models stay cloud choices); **Mixed Media** — 33 looks for 1–10 s clips with per-preset settings and a preview frame, 14 of them with a LoRA; the clip's frames are sampled at the rate you pick (4–24 FPS) and **Per frame** redraws each one on Fuobe Mixed Media by default on both engines (Nano Banana 2 and Seedream 5.0 Flash stay cloud choices, priced per frame), **Smooth** draws the first frame and carries it through on both engines, and **Fast** restyles in one video-to-video pass in the cloud. In the cloud, Smooth and Fuobe Shorts refuse a selection under 17 frames or a clip under 5 fps at the estimate, before anything is paid for
- **Genjutsu** — keep a 4–30 s clip's motion, camera and framing and change what is in it: motion transfer, **Character swap**, object swap, restyle (44 looks, Fuobe Soul's 139 or your own images) or insert, with reference images, priced before it runs. Every mode but Insert defaults to a Fuobe Original on both engines, and the picker shows each model's source (Original, open weights or partner) and licence. Character swap turns the person in a 5–15 s part of one shot (up to 30 s, in pieces or windows) into your character from one image, keeping the performance and the sound: pick who (largest, leftmost, rightmost, a number or, on your GPU, a click), 480p or 768p, and by default your character goes into the first frame first; "Describe my character" and "Use the clip's words" help write the prompt. For whole clips, **Open in Character Swap Studio** takes the clip and the character there
- **Character Swap Studio** (`/character-swap`) — Akatz Labs' H3 Character Swap workbench, rebuilt in Fuobe on MiniMax H3: put your character into whole clips, shot by shot, and get each clip back with its own timing and sound. A project holds one character (a picture and a description) and up to 20 clips (.mp4, .mov, .mkv, .webm or .avi, each up to 149.66 s and 200 MB), worked through seven stages: **Shots** (Detect edits finds the cuts in your browser; mark each shot Replace character or Keep original), **Words** (transcribe, say who speaks each line, build the shot prompts), **Who to swap** (largest, leftmost, rightmost, person #n or words, plus clicks on any frame, giving mask versions you approve), **Scene anchors** (make or import a still of the scene with your character in it), **Segments** (5, 10 or 15 s, each starting fresh or continuing the one before; a continuation starts itself when that take is done, even with the page closed), **Takes & review** (several takes, Compare, Keep / Maybe / Skip with notes, Export decisions) and **Assemble** (the chosen takes over the original timeline with the clip's sound, to download and in the Library). Prices show before anything runs (only a cloud browser copy of a clip your browser can't play, and anchors on GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst, which bill by tokens, are left out of the estimate), and demo mode runs the whole flow for free. On your GPU, H3 Relay 2.0 swaps only the person in your approved mask, matches the anchor and continues from the take before (free; a 24 GB GPU and the opt-in H3 Relay pack). In the cloud, fal's MiniMax H3 with Akatz Labs' swap LoRA ($0.075/s at 768p, $0.0625/s at 480p) has no mask: the whole frame is regenerated and who to swap goes into the prompt. Built and checked in demo mode only: nothing has run on a GPU or with a fal key, its two GPU graphs have never loaded in a ComfyUI, and the Finish stage (a clean background plate and cut-outs) shows as not available
- **Marketing Studio** (84 templates; every picture format starts on Fuobe Marketing Image on both engines, with the words set over the picture in your browser, and Bria, Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image 2.5, Ideogram and Recraft stay cloud choices), **Click to Ad** (with a logo end card), **Ads Studio** (brand kit from your website, up to 20 ads per product in 1:1 / 4:5 / 9:16, each one written copy and one picture laid out in your browser with your logo, colours and fonts; the picture is Fuobe Marketing Image's by default, and in the cloud "Pictures by" picks Nano Banana Pro or GPT Image 2.5 instead) and **Ad Multiplier** (whole-ad edits in the cloud on Kling O3 or Happy Horse video edit, which keep the sound and recreate up to 15 s, or Gemini Omni Flash)
